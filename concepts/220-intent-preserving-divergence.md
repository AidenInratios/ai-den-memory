# 220 – Intent-Preserving Divergence

Status: proposed
Verification: pending
Principles: P1, P3, P5, P6, P7, P8, P13
Tags: co-creation, divergence, provenance, interpretation, growth-medium

## Summary
AI-den’s co-creative value can arise when a human seed idea and the AI’s adjacent interpretation are both preserved rather than collapsed into one “correct” meaning. A misunderstood or reframed input can generate a useful new branch, provided the original human intent remains traceable and is not overwritten. The productive unit is therefore not agreement alone, but lineage-preserving divergence.

## Details
Problem: AI interpretation can distort human intent. Conventional assistants often silently normalize the input into the model’s preferred framing, losing the original thought. Yet some divergences produce genuinely useful adjacent concepts.

Proposal: Preserve both branches explicitly:

Human seed A → intended meaning
AI interpretation A′ → adjacent derived meaning
A ↔ A′ → comparison / relational delta → possible new B

When the human corrects the AI, the correction should not erase A′ if A′ produced reusable value. Instead, provenance should show which meaning originated from the human and which was generated by the AI. RST can examine the relational delta between A and A′; CIP protects human agency, authorship, and the right to reject the derived branch.

Risks: romanticizing misunderstanding; attributing AI-generated content to the human; drifting away from the user’s actual goal; multiplying branches until memory becomes noisy; or treating novelty as value without verification.

## Principles
- P1 Relational Ontology: meaning can emerge in the relation between intended and interpreted forms.
- P3 Recursive Transparency: preserve provenance of the original seed and derived branch.
- P5 Triple Verification: useful divergence still requires factual, contextual, and ethical checking.
- P6 Symmetrical Co-creation: AI may extend but must not overwrite human agency.
- P7 Dialogic Resonance: correction can become generative rather than merely error repair.
- P8 Dynamic Equilibrium: preserve useful variation while preventing uncontrolled drift.
- P13 Resonant Ethics: co-creation remains legitimate only when provenance and agency are respected.
